# Corrine's Final Exam ✈️ — The Check Ride

**20 questions · No multiple choice · No hints · Codes are UPPERCASE, exactly 3 letters**

This is the hardest test in the Academy. It is free-recall on purpose — real crew
scheduling never gives you four options to pick from. Write your answers on a
separate sheet, then grade yourself against the key at the bottom. **Passing is 18/20.**
Anything less and a destination is still hiding from you.

> Scoring note: several questions have multiple parts. You only get the point if you
> nail **every** part. Half-right is wrong at 35,000 feet.

---

## Level 1 — Warm-Up (still not easy)

**1.** Give the 3-letter code for each hub, in this order:
Chicago O'Hare, Denver, Houston (Bush), Newark, San Francisco, Los Angeles,
Washington Dulles, and the one hub that isn't in the continental U.S.

**2.** New York City has three of our airports. Name all three codes **and** say which
of them is a United **hub**.

**3.** Two of our airports serve Dallas. Give both codes and the full airport name each
one stands for.

**4.** Name the city for each: **PDX**, **MSY**, **BNA**, **RDU**, **SDF**.

**5.** Chicago and Washington each have two of our airports. Give all four codes and
label which city each belongs to.

---

## Level 2 — Cruising Altitude

**6.** Hawaii has five destinations on our map. Give the code for **each island's
airport** and name the island (not just the city): Oahu, Maui, Kauai, Kona side of
the Big Island, and Hilo side of the Big Island.

**7.** Canada's codes all start with the same letter. Give the code for Toronto,
Vancouver, Montreal, Calgary, and Ottawa — **and** say which letter they all share
and why.

**8.** Tokyo has two of our airports. Give both codes, the full name of each, and say
which one sits closer to downtown.

**9.** Name the country for each code: **TLV**, **DOH**, **AMM**, **DXB**, **KEF**.

**10.** These codes do **not** spell their city. Name the city for each:
**GRU**, **GIG**, **EZE**, **SCL**, **UIO**.

---

## Level 3 — The Trainer Is Trying to Fail You

**11.** **SJC** and **SJO** are one letter apart and both mean "San Jose." Give the
city, **state or country**, and region (Domestic vs. International) for each.

**12.** For each of these tricky codes, give the U.S. city — no partial credit:
**MCO**, **MCI**, **CVG**, **GEG**, **FAT**.

**13.** Go city → code. Give the code for: **Sacramento**, **Santa Ana / Orange
County**, **Fort Myers**, **West Palm Beach**, **Bozeman**.

**14.** Central America, city → code: **Panama City**, **San José, Costa Rica**,
**Guatemala City**, **San Salvador**, **Belize City**. (Careful — one of these looks a
lot like a question-11 answer.)

**15.** Match each code to its **country**, not its city:
**MBJ**, **PUJ**, **SDQ**, **AUA**, **STT**.

---

## Level 4 — Purser-Level (brutal)

**16.** Europe, code → city, rapid fire:
**CDG**, **FCO**, **MXP**, **MUC**, **BCN**, **ZRH**, **LIS**, **EDI**.

**17.** East Asia, code → city (and note the two that are in **China**):
**NRT**, **ICN**, **PVG**, **PEK**, **HKG**, **TPE**.

**18.** Some codes carry a story. For **ORD**, **MCO**, and **MSY**, name the city
**and** the historical reason the code isn't obvious (the memory hook).

**19.** South & Southeast Asia + Oceania, city → code:
**Singapore**, **Manila**, **Delhi**, **Mumbai**, **Bangkok**, **Sydney**,
**Auckland**. Then: which one of those is **not** in Australia but is often confused
with it, and what country is it in?

**20.** The Lightning Round — give the code, and no thinking out loud:
Reykjavik, Tel Aviv, Cape Town, Johannesburg, Punta Cana, Los Cabos, Cozumel,
Guadalajara, Quito, Lima, Bogotá, and Guam. **(All twelve, or the question is zero.)**

# ANSWER KEY

**1.** ORD, DEN, IAH, EWR, SFO, LAX, IAD — and **GUM (Guam)**, the non-continental hub.

**2.** **JFK** (New York JFK), **LGA** (LaGuardia), **EWR** (Newark). **EWR is the
hub.** (JFK and LGA are flown but Newark is United's New York hub.)

**3.** **DAL** = Dallas Love Field · **DFW** = Dallas/Fort Worth.

**4.** PDX = Portland (OR) · MSY = New Orleans · BNA = Nashville · RDU =
Raleigh/Durham · SDF = Louisville.

**5.** Chicago: **ORD** (O'Hare) & **MDW** (Midway). Washington: **IAD** (Dulles) &
**DCA** (Reagan).

**6.** Oahu = **HNL** (Honolulu) · Maui = **OGG** (Kahului) · Kauai = **LIH** (Lihue)
· Kona/Big Island = **KOA** (Kona) · Hilo/Big Island = **ITO** (Hilo).

**7.** Toronto **YYZ**, Vancouver **YVR**, Montreal **YUL**, Calgary **YYC**, Ottawa
**YOW**. They all start with **Y** — Canada's airport codes are prefixed with Y.

**8.** **NRT** = Tokyo Narita (the big international field, farther out) · **HND** =
Tokyo Haneda (**closer to downtown**).

**9.** TLV = Israel · DOH = Qatar · AMM = Jordan · DXB = United Arab Emirates · KEF =
Iceland.

**10.** GRU = São Paulo · GIG = Rio de Janeiro · EZE = Buenos Aires · SCL = Santiago
· UIO = Quito.

**11.** **SJC** = San Jose, **California**, **Domestic**. **SJO** = San José, **Costa
Rica**, **International**.

**12.** MCO = Orlando · MCI = Kansas City · CVG = Cincinnati · GEG = Spokane · FAT =
Fresno.

**13.** Sacramento **SMF** · Santa Ana/Orange County **SNA** · Fort Myers **RSW** ·
West Palm Beach **PBI** · Bozeman **BZN**.

**14.** Panama City **PTY** · San José, Costa Rica **SJO** · Guatemala City **GUA** ·
San Salvador **SAL** · Belize City **BZE**. (The look-alike is **SJO** from Q11.)

**15.** MBJ = Jamaica (Montego Bay) · PUJ = Dominican Republic (Punta Cana) · SDQ =
Dominican Republic (Santo Domingo) · AUA = Aruba · STT = U.S. Virgin Islands
(St. Thomas).

**16.** CDG = Paris · FCO = Rome · MXP = Milan · MUC = Munich · BCN = Barcelona · ZRH
= Zurich · LIS = Lisbon · EDI = Edinburgh.

**17.** NRT = Tokyo (Narita) · ICN = Seoul · PVG = Shanghai · PEK = Beijing · HKG =
Hong Kong · TPE = Taipei. **The two in China: PVG (Shanghai) and PEK (Beijing).**

**18.** ORD = Chicago O'Hare — from **ORcharD Field**, the airfield's original name.
MCO = Orlando — from **McCoy** Air Force Base. MSY = New Orleans — from the old
**Moisant** Stock Yards airfield.

**19.** Singapore **SIN** · Manila **MNL** · Delhi **DEL** · Mumbai **BOM** · Bangkok
**BKK** · Sydney **SYD** · Auckland **AKL**. **AKL (Auckland) is not in Australia —
it's in New Zealand.**

**20.** Reykjavik **KEF** · Tel Aviv **TLV** · Cape Town **CPT** · Johannesburg **JNB**
· Punta Cana **PUJ** · Los Cabos **SJD** · Cozumel **CZM** · Guadalajara **GDL** ·
Quito **UIO** · Lima **LIM** · Bogotá **BOG** · Guam **GUM**.

### How'd she do?

- **20/20** — Chief Purser 🏆. She knows the map cold.
- **18–19** — Cleared to fly. Circle the misses and re-run those routes.
- **15–17** — Close, but a passenger will catch her. Back to the Standby flights.
- **< 15** — More reps needed. The confusable pairs (Q11, Q14) and the story codes
  (Q18) are where most people bleed points.
