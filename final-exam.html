1
Give the code for each hub, in order: Chicago O'Hare, Denver, Houston (Bush), Newark, San Francisco, Los Angeles, Washington Dulles, and the one hub that isn't in the continental U.S.
Answer
ORD DEN IAH EWR SFO LAX IAD — and GUM (Guam), the non-continental hub.
2
New York City has three of our airports. Name all three codes and say which one is United's hub.
Answer
JFK (New York JFK), LGA (LaGuardia), EWR (Newark). EWR is the hub.
3
Two of our airports serve Dallas. Give both codes and the full airport name each stands for.
Answer
DAL = Dallas Love Field · DFW = Dallas/Fort Worth.
4
Name the city for each: PDX, MSY, BNA, RDU, SDF.
Answer
PDX = Portland (OR) · MSY = New Orleans · BNA = Nashville · RDU = Raleigh/Durham · SDF = Louisville.
5
Chicago and Washington each have two of our airports. Give all four codes and label which city each belongs to.
Answer
Chicago: ORD (O'Hare) & MDW (Midway). Washington: IAD (Dulles) & DCA (Reagan).

Cruising Altitude

The confusable clusters.
6
Hawaii has five destinations. Give the airport code for each island and name the island: Oahu, Maui, Kauai, the Kona side of the Big Island, and the Hilo side of the Big Island.
Answer
Oahu = HNL (Honolulu) · Maui = OGG (Kahului) · Kauai = LIH (Lihue) · Kona = KOA · Hilo = ITO.
7
Canada's codes share one starting letter. Give the code for Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal, Calgary, and Ottawa — and the letter they share.
Answer
Toronto YYZ, Vancouver YVR, Montreal YUL, Calgary YYC, Ottawa YOW. They all start with Y.
8
Tokyo has two airports. Give both codes, the full name of each, and say which sits closer to downtown.
Answer
NRT = Tokyo Narita (big international field, farther out) · HND = Tokyo Haneda (closer to downtown).
9
Name the country for each: TLV, DOH, AMM, DXB, KEF.
Answer
TLV = Israel · DOH = Qatar · AMM = Jordan · DXB = United Arab Emirates · KEF = Iceland.
10
These codes don't spell their city. Name the city for each: GRU, GIG, EZE, SCL, UIO.
Answer
GRU = São Paulo · GIG = Rio de Janeiro · EZE = Buenos Aires · SCL = Santiago · UIO = Quito.

18
Some codes carry a story. For ORD, MCO, and MSY, name the city and the historical reason the code isn't obvious.
Answer
ORD = Chicago O'Hare — from ORcharD Field. MCO = Orlando — from McCoy Air Force Base. MSY = New Orleans — from the old Moisant Stock Yards airfield.

Answer
Singapore SIN · Manila MNL · Delhi DEL · Mumbai BOM · Bangkok BKK · Sydney SYD · Auckland AKL. AKL is in New Zealand, not Australia.
